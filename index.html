Feeding Timer

A shared nursing and bottle log for two parents.

Start a nursing timer on the left or right with one tap, log bottles, see how long since the last feed started and which side is next. Share the log with your partner and either of you can start a timer that the other finishes.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email t.bragg@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working.

Sharing with your partner

In the app, open Settings (the gear) → Invite your partner, and send the link in Messages. When they open it the app joins their phone to your log. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.

Reminders

Settings → Reminders. “Time for a feed” can use a fixed interval or learn your typical daytime gap; quiet hours keep the overnight stretch out of it. “Timer left running” nudges you if a nursing timer runs past the usual length. Both are off until you turn them on.

Privacy

Feeding Timer collects no data. Your log lives in your own iCloud and is shared only with the people you invite. See the privacy policy.
