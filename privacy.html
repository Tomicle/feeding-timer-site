Privacy Policy

Feeding Timer, by Thomas Bragg. Effective 8 November 2026.

The short version

Feeding Timer does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers run by us.

What the app stores

The app records feeds you log: when a nursing or bottle feed started, how long each side lasted, bottle amounts and contents, and any notes you type. This information is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit. We cannot read it; Apple’s privacy policy governs iCloud.

Sharing

If you invite a partner, Apple’s iCloud sharing gives their Apple Account access to your feeding log. You can see who has access and remove them at any time in the app’s Settings. Nobody else can see the log.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to Apple to deliver them.

Widget

The Lock Screen widget reads a small summary (time of the last feed, which side is next) that the app stores on your phone. It leaves your phone only through your own iCloud backup, if you use one.

Children

The app is used by parents and records information about feeding an infant. It does not collect any information from children and has no accounts or communication features.

Deleting your data

Delete feeds in the app, or delete the app and remove its data from iCloud in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
